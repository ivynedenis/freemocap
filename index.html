Built for South Africa

Local strengths that foreign sellers lack

Twelve official languages

Creators and AI hosts who sell in isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sesotho and English reach buyers global brands miss.

WhatsApp shopping habit

Sell through WhatsApp and Instagram lives today, then move those buyers to TikTok Shop on launch day.

Stokvels

Community savings clubs already buy in bulk together, a ready made base for group buying.

Pickup points

Pargo and PAXI at PEP stores give low cost delivery and returns far beyond the metros.

Local payments

Ozow, PayFast, Yoco, SnapScan and Peach Payments take instant EFT and card payments for live sales before TikTok checkout arrives.

Payday rhythm

Month end around the 25th, Black Friday and Heritage Day give a calendar for named sale events.

Power resilience

Studios with inverter and solar backup can promise sellers streams that stay live through outages.

Proudly South African products

Rooibos, marula skincare, braai goods, wigs and braids, local fashion: stories that sell at premium prices.

Sequence

A plan for one founder or a small team

Now to month 3

Readiness agency and WhatsApp selling

Sign five local brands, run their creator campaigns on TikTok One, and earn from WhatsApp comment to buy lives while TikTok Shop is pending.

Months 3 to 9

Studio, host academy and sample hub

Open one studio with power backup, train hosts in several languages, and link samples to Pargo and PAXI points.

Launch day

Switch on checkout

Move every client, creator and buyer list onto TikTok Shop in the first week, when launch subsidies are richest.

Months 9 to 18

Data tool and AI hosts

Turn agency data into a local analytics product and test AI hosts on overnight streams.

Month 12 onward

Own brand

Launch a private label in the category your data shows selling best. Hair and beauty is the strongest candidate.
